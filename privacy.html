Privacy.

This notice explains how information is handled when you visit the Wolfgang Douglas profile website.

Browsing the website

The website itself does not contain a contact form, analytics scripts, advertising trackers or a newsletter signup. The hosting provider may process technical information such as IP addresses, browser information and request logs to deliver and protect the service. Hosting access or security features may use essential cookies.

Email enquiries

Email links open your own email application. If you send an enquiry, the recipient team receives the details you choose to include and uses them to respond. For questions about handling your enquiry or a request concerning your personal information, contact team@wolfmba.com.

Videos and external links

Video thumbnails are hosted with this website. A YouTube player is only loaded after you select a video and choose “Load YouTube video”. At that point, YouTube may process your IP address and device information and may use cookies. The player uses YouTube’s privacy-enhanced embed address.

Links to LinkedIn, YouTube, Romanichal and Wolf MBA take you to external websites. Their own privacy notices apply when you use those services. Read Google’s privacy policy for YouTube’s handling of information.

Questions

Contact team@wolfmba.com with privacy questions about this website. Include enough information for the team to understand your request.
